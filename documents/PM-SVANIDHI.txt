MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
SCHEME GUIDELINES: PM STREET VENDOR'S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI)
Notification: K-12017/2/2020 | Version: 2.5-2026 | Last Updated: 2026-03-01
Applicability: Urban Areas across All States and Union Territories

1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
1.1 PM SVANidhi is a Central Sector Scheme facilitating affordable working capital loans to street vendors to resume their livelihoods in urban and peri-urban areas.
1.2 Credit Tranches:
    - 1st Tranche: Collateral-free working capital loan up to Rs. 10,000/- for a 1-year tenure.
    - 2nd Tranche: Up to Rs. 20,000/- on timely or early repayment of the 1st loan.
    - 3rd Tranche: Up to Rs. 50,000/- on successful completion of the 2nd loan.
1.3 Incentives:
    - 7% per annum interest subsidy credited directly to bank account on a quarterly basis.
    - Monthly cashback incentive up to Rs. 100/- (Rs. 1,200/- annually) for digital transactions.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
2.1 Target Occupation: Street vendors, hawkers, thelewala, rehris, vegetable/fruit sellers, tea stalls, and mobile cobblers engaged in vending in urban areas.
2.2 Age: 18 years or above.
2.3 Household Income Ceiling: Up to Rs. 3,00,000/- per annum.
2.4 Location: Operating in urban local bodies (Municipalities / Corporations).
2.5 Identification Proof: Must possess a Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by the Town Vending Committee (TVC) or Urban Local Body (ULB).

3. EXCLUSIONS
3.1 Non-urban vending or non-vending businesses are not eligible.
3.2 Institutional or corporate retail establishments are strictly barred.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
4.1 Aadhaar Card linked to mobile number.
4.2 Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by Urban Local Body.
4.3 Active Savings Bank Account Passbook with IFSC code.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS
5.1 Apply online on https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or through Urban Local Body desks / CSCs.
5.2 Loan sanctioned and disbursed directly within 7 to 10 days by partner Scheduled Commercial Banks, RRBs, or NBFC-MFIs.
