DEPARTMENT OF POSTS / MINISTRY OF FINANCE - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (SSY) - STATUTORY SCHEME GUIDELINES
Notification: GSR 914(E) | Version: 2.0-2026 | Last Updated: 2026-03-01
Applicability: Pan-India (All Post Offices and Authorized Banks)

1. OBJECTIVE AND HIGHLIGHTS
1.1 Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA) is a government-backed savings scheme aimed at the welfare, higher education, and marriage expenses of girl children under the 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' campaign.
1.2 Highlights:
    - Sovereign-guaranteed high compound interest rate (8.2%+ per annum).
    - Triple Exempt (EEE) tax status under Section 80C (investment, interest, and maturity proceeds are 100% tax-free).
    - Long-term maturity corpus exceeding Rs. 15 to 25 Lakhs based on annual contribution.

2. ELIGIBILITY RULES
2.1 Beneficiary: Account can be opened only in the name of a girl child from the date of her birth until she attains the age of 10 years.
2.2 Guardian: Account opened and operated by natural or legal guardian until child attains 18 years.
2.3 Account Limits: Only one account allowed per girl child, and maximum two accounts per family (with exception for twins/triplets).
2.4 Location & Income: Pan-India, open to all income categories.

3. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS
3.1 Birth Certificate of the Girl Child issued by Municipal Registrar.
3.2 Aadhaar Card / Identity Proof of Parent or Legal Guardian.
3.3 PAN Card of Guardian (or Form 60).
3.4 Initial deposit receipt (minimum Rs. 250/-).

4. HOW TO OPEN ACCOUNT
4.1 Submit Form-1 at any Post Office or authorized commercial bank branch.
4.2 Deposit between Rs. 250/- and Rs. 1,50,000/- per financial year for up to 15 years.
4.3 Account matures after 21 years from the date of opening, with partial withdrawal (up to 50%) permitted for higher education after girl turns 18.
4.4 Official scheme portal: https://www.indiapost.gov.in
