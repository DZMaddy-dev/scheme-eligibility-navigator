MINISTRY OF RURAL DEVELOPMENT - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
PROGRAMME GUIDELINES: PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA - GRAMIN (PMAY-G)
Reference Framework: G-31011/2/2016 | Version: 2.0-2026 | Last Updated: 2026-03-01
Applicability: All Rural Areas Across States & Union Territories

1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
1.1 PMAY-G aims to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all houseless households and households living in kutcha and dilapidated houses in rural areas.
1.2 Unit assistance provided: Rs. 1,20,000/- in plain areas and Rs. 1,30,000/- in hilly, difficult, and Integrated Action Plan (IAP) districts.
1.3 In addition, beneficiaries receive 90/95 person-days of unskilled wage employment under MGNREGA amounting to approx Rs. 27,000/-, plus Rs. 12,000/- for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin (SBM-G).

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
2.1 Rural Houseless / Kutcha Dwelling: Must be living in a kutcha house (walls and roof made of non-permanent materials) or be completely houseless.
2.2 Annual Household Income: Must not exceed Rs. 1,80,000/- per annum.
2.3 Age Requirement: Applicant must be 18 years or older.
2.4 Location: Must reside in a declared rural area under Gram Panchayat jurisdiction.

3. 13-POINT STATUTORY EXCLUSION CRITERIA
Any household possessing any of the following assets/conditions is automatically disqualified:
(i) Motorized two/three/four wheeler or fishing boat.
(ii) Mechanized three/four wheeler agricultural equipment.
(iii) Kisan Credit Card with credit limit of Rs. 50,000 or above.
(iv) Any member who is a government employee.
(v) Households with non-agricultural enterprises registered with Government.
(vi) Any member paying income tax or professional tax.
(vii) Already possessing a permanent pucca house with more than 2 rooms.
(viii) Owning 2.5 acres or more of irrigated land with at least one irrigation equipment.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
4.1 Aadhaar Card of the applicant and adult household members.
4.2 Active Bank / Post Office Savings Account Passbook linked to Aadhaar.
4.3 MGNREGA Job Card Number to claim 90-day construction wage support.
4.4 Geo-tagged photograph of the existing dilapidated dwelling site certified by Panchayat Secretary.

5. DISBURSEMENT WORKFLOW
5.1 Selection from Awaas+ waitlist verified by the Gram Sabha.
5.2 Assistance disbursed in three milestone-based installments verified via the AwaasApp geo-tagged photographic inspection.
5.3 Official citizen information portal: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmayg
